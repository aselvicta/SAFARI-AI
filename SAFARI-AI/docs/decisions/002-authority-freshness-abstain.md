# Authority, freshness, and abstention are retrieval rules

Money, entry, and safety answers are easy to overstate. The generator therefore has hard gates on top of the weighted score:

- A fee amount is taken only from a structured row on an official document whose effective window covers the question date.
- A superseded row can explain that an older tariff is in the index. Its amount is omitted.
- Species, camping, vehicles, child entry, seasons, and breakdown procedure are answered only when a non-profile passage supports them. Park profiles state area, year, and planning tags. They do not state wildlife lists or rules.
- Ngorongoro, Zanzibar, Mafia, and visa questions are pointed at the authority that owns them. Phase 1 does not invent that authority's rules.
- Planning shortlists are labeled MEDIUM confidence because they are a filter, not an itinerary.

Hashed embeddings will miss a paraphrase that shares no tokens. BM25 and the metadata bonuses cover the questions in the evaluation file. A trained embedding model is a later swap, at dimension 384 or with a column migration.
